## 2. Hook

**Chosen (no reorder):** the opening line is already the strongest statement in the footage: a
recognisable sales tactic, a warning, and an implied "so who decides?" that the payoff answers at 22.7 s.
Frame 0 carries the claim in his own words, "SA CAKAP LOAN KAU BOLEH APPROVE?", over the Perodua wall,
so the thumbnail works muted. The claim is marked lime on "boleh approve" (3.1 s); a red HATI-HATI
stamp lands on "kena hati-hati" (5.46 s).

**Alternatives considered, not built:**
- B: cold-open on "Keputusan akhir terletak daripada tangan bank" (1.7 s), then the hook. Stronger
  authority, but it gives away the payoff before the setup.
- C: cold-open on "Aku tak nak bagi harapan palsu" (2 s). Strong trust line, but out of context it
  reads as an answer to a question nobody asked yet.
